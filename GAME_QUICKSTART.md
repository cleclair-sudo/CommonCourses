# Data Center Mayor Game - Quick Start Guide

## How to Play

### Objective
Manage a city's response to a new AI data center. Make 5 key decisions to balance citizen happiness, environmental quality, budget, and resources.

### Game Flow
1. **Start**: View the full city map with roads, buildings, and moving citizens
2. **Click**: Click on any building to see decision options specific to that area
3. **Decide**: Choose from 3 options - each has different impacts
4. **Impact**: Watch the stats change and see the effects on your city
5. **Repeat**: Make 4 more decisions (5 turns total)
6. **End**: Game ends with a grade based on your overall performance

### Reading the City

**Buildings You'll See:**
- 🏠 Houses/Apartments - Where citizens live
- 📡 Data Center - The AI facility (red, polluting)
- 💧 Water Treatment - Manages water resources
- ⚡ Power Station - Provides electricity
- 🏫 Schools - Education for youth
- 🏛️ Town Hall - Government center
- 🏬 Shopping Center - Commercial hub
- 🏢 Office Park - Business district
- 🏥 Hospital - Medical services

**Animated People:**
- Small pink dots moving around = your citizens
- Emoji above each person shows their mood:
  - 😊 Happy (when happiness > 60%)
  - 😐 Neutral (happiness 30-60%)
  - 😞 Unhappy (happiness < 30%)

**Roads:**
- Gray grid pattern = road system
- Dashed centerlines = lane markings
- Citizens walk on these roads

### Understanding Your Stats

| Stat | What It Means | Starting Value |
|------|--------------|-----------------|
| 😊 Happiness | How happy your citizens are (0-100%) | 75% |
| 💰 Budget | Your city's money in thousands | $500K |
| 🌍 Environment | Air/water quality (0-100%) | 60% |
| 🔋 Resources | Resource efficiency (0-100%) | 40% |

### Decision Examples

#### When You Click the Data Center
- **Allow Expansion**: More jobs (+happiness) but more pollution (-environment)
- **Cap Growth**: Keep it smaller (less impact either way)
- **Relocate**: Remove it entirely (happy citizens, budget hit)

#### When You Click Residential Areas
- **Build Noise Barriers**: Reduce pollution impact on residents
- **Support Community**: Build community programs for satisfaction
- **Lower Taxes**: Give tax breaks to residents

#### When You Click the Water Treatment Plant
- **Build Treatment**: Better water quality but costs budget
- **Use Grid**: Minimal cost but less control
- **Partial System**: Balanced middle ground

### Tips for Success

✅ **Balance is Key**
- Don't just maximize happiness; consider environment too
- Budget runs out fast with big projects
- Resources and happiness are interconnected

✅ **Think Ahead**
- You only have 5 decisions - plan your moves
- Early decisions affect options later
- Some buildings need attention more than others

✅ **Watch Your Citizens**
- Happy citizens (😊) = good decisions
- Unhappy citizens (😞) = time for community support
- Mix of emotions shows balanced governance

✅ **Environmental Awareness**
- Pollution overlay gets visible when environment < 50%
- Decisions with green particles are better for environment
- Decisions with red particles may harm the environment

### Scoring

After 5 turns, you get a grade based on average of:
- Citizen Happiness
- Environment Quality  
- Resource Efficiency

| Average Score | Result | Emoji |
|----------------|--------|-------|
| > 70% | Excellent Mayor! | 🎉 |
| 40-70% | Good Management! | 😊 |
| < 40% | Mixed Results | 😐 |
| < 30% happiness OR negative budget | Citizens Revolted! | 😡 |

### Game Over
When you reach the Game Over screen:
- See your final grade
- View the outcome message
- Click "Play Again" to try new strategies
- Click "Back to Courses" to return to course menu

## Common Strategies

### The "Green" Approach
Focus on environment and sustainability:
- Relocate the data center
- Build water treatment systems
- Go solar for power
- Support the community
- **Result**: High environment, may hurt budget/happiness

### The "People First" Approach
Focus on happiness and community:
- Build noise barriers
- Support community programs
- Create jobs
- Lower taxes
- **Result**: Happy citizens, may hurt environment/budget

### The "Balanced" Approach
Try to keep all stats mid-range:
- Cap data center growth
- Partial water system
- Hybrid energy
- Build some programs, lower some taxes
- **Result**: Mixed but stable scores

### The "Economic" Approach
Focus on budget and jobs:
- Allow data center expansion
- Tax the data center
- Create jobs
- Use grid power (cheap)
- **Result**: Good budget, may hurt happiness/environment

## Clicking Tips

**To select a building:**
1. Move your mouse over the building
2. Watch for the golden border highlight
3. Click when you see the building light up
4. Decision options appear in the right sidebar

**If clicking isn't working:**
- Make sure you're clicking on the building itself, not the name below
- Try clicking on the center of the building
- Refresh the page if the game becomes unresponsive

## About the Real World

This game is based on **real issues** that communities face with AI data centers:

🔴 **Real Concerns:**
- Google's data center in The Dalles, Oregon uses massive amounts of water
- Noise complaints from communities near Meta/Google facilities
- Power grid strain from electricity demands
- Job creation vs. environmental impact debates

🟢 **Real Benefits:**
- Thousands of construction and permanent jobs
- Tax revenue for local governments
- Technological advancement and innovation
- Infrastructure improvements

💡 **The Point:**
There's rarely a "perfect" solution. Real mayors make these kinds of tradeoffs every day. This game helps you understand the complexity!
